Framework coverage

Control mapping is a review aid; applicability must be confirmed for each organization.

DPDP Act, 2023Primary mapping · staged commencement: substantive duties are scheduled 18 months after 13 Nov 2025. Evidence here is a support tool, not a compliance finding.
CERT-In Directions, 2022Specified Annexure-I cyber incidents have a six-hour reporting requirement for covered entities. SentryAI activity is a detection signal, not by itself a confirmed reportable breach. Draft captures discovery time, impact estimate, category and containment; owner must assess and submit. CERT-In FAQ
RBI payment-data direction · IT outsourcingFor covered payment-system providers, payment-system data must be stored in India; RBI IT-outsourcing directions apply to specified regulated entities, including NBFCs. This MVP flags destination as unverified; it cannot prove vendor geography or localization. Not a blanket localization claim for every fintech or financial record.
IRDAI 2023 Information & Cyber SecurityInsurance regulated-entity scope · this MVP supplies monitored AI-use events and an internal incident draft for review; applicability and required reporting must be confirmed against current IRDAI circulars.
SEBI CSCRF, 2024SEBI-regulated entity scope · event monitoring and incident-record evidence can support review; it does not replace SOC, incident portal submission, RCA or other CSCRF controls.
India AI Governance Guidelines (MeitY, 5 Nov 2025)Released as a principle-based national framework, not a new statutory compliance certificate. Partial alignment evidence: accountable AI-use policy, privacy/risk alerts, human approval and auditable decisions. No model fairness, explainability or safety evaluation is performed by this MVP.

This MVP does not certify compliance. Destination country is unverified unless supported by vendor and account-specific evidence.
